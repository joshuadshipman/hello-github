## 1. Executive Summary & Root-Cause Elimination

### The Critical Failure in Previous Builds:
1. **Targeting Blindness (Violating T1.25h):** Previous pages exclusively pitched "8-to-25+ Truck Enterprise Fleets" with corporate jargon. A solo plumber in **Mena, AR**, **Broken Bow, OK**, or outside **Marfa, TX** has 1 to 2 trucks, is physically under a crawlspace or sink when the phone rings, and cannot afford a full-time dispatcher. Pitching him "enterprise workforce management" repels him instantly.
2. **Excessive Text Density (>40% Text Slop):** The page presented dense walls of marketing copy instead of high-converting visual infographics, interactive calculators, and step-by-step pictorial flows.
3. **Headset Master & Positioning Defects (Directive B2):** Visuals failed to use the pinned 3D Headset Master (`AGENTANGEL_HEADSET_HALO_MASTER.blend`) at seated headrest position (`Y = seated head, facing desk monitors at a 3/4 angle`).
4. **Clone Slop:** Plumbing copy was cloned to roofing and HVAC without trade-specific friction.
